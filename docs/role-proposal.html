A proposed role to own WeLearn Growth Hub from pilot through to production

Proposed by: Mani Golzary  |  Date: 11 September 2026
Accompanies: WeLearn Growth Hub — Project Summary

Why this role, and why now

WeLearn Growth Hub was built outside my current remit, on my own time and my own tooling. That was the right way to prove the idea — it cost the organisation nothing and carried no risk. It is not a sustainable way to run the platform from here.

If the pilot proceeds, the Growth Hub stops being a demonstration and becomes software holding real student records. Software in that position needs a named owner: someone accountable for the roadmap, for the data it holds, for the teachers depending on it, and for the decisions that get made about it. No existing role at the organisation covers that, and it is not something that should sit informally with whoever happens to have built it.

This proposal sets out a role that formalises the work already happening, and defines the terms on which it continues.

What the role covers

AreaResponsibility
Platform ownershipRoadmap, build, maintenance and reliability of the Growth Hub. Accountable for the security of student data held within it.
Learning designProject-based learning frameworks, goal-setting methodology, and accessibility provision for twice-exceptional and additional-needs learners.
Coach enablementTraining teachers to use the platform, gathering their feedback, and translating it into product decisions.
Applied AIEvaluating and integrating AI capability across teaching and learning — the platform is the first application, not the only one.
Marketing strategyContinues as today, including positioning the platform externally should the organisation choose to.

What changes from my current position

Marketing strategy and PBL coaching remain. What is added is formal accountability for a production system, and the protected time to discharge it properly. Three things need to be agreed for that to work:

1. Time allocation

A defined split between platform work and existing duties — I would propose [x days per week] on the Growth Hub during the pilot and build phase, reviewed once it is stable. Without a protected allocation this continues to run on personal evenings, which does not scale and creates a single point of failure for the organisation.

2. Infrastructure on organisational accounts

The platform currently runs on my personal AI subscriptions. Before it touches real student data, the AI services must move onto organisational accounts — this is a governance requirement rather than a financial one. Running cost for a 30-student cohort is approximately $10–15 per month; the detail is in Section 7 of the accompanying summary.

3. Compensation

The work to date has been absorbed personally, and I am not seeking payment retrospectively for the prototype. What I am asking is that the work from the pilot onward is contracted and compensated — either as a revised position, or as a defined project engagement for the ten-week programme.

For context on scale

The existing platform represents roughly 20–33 developer-weeks of work: five interactive simulations, an authentication and role system, a data layer with security rules, a coach portal, a goal-setting system, and AI integration throughout. Commissioned externally, that is in the region of $50,000–130,000 depending on the supplier. The remaining ten-week programme is of a similar order. I offer these figures as a measure of what has been delivered, not as an invoice.

What I would commit to

MilestoneMeasure
Pilot completedOne class runs the platform end to end, with genuine learning data recorded and the teacher's assessment documented.
Teacher adoptionCoaches using the portal weekly without support, evidenced by feedback and ratings actually being given.
ReliabilityNo data loss; no security incident; dashboard responsive at full cohort size.
Readiness for scaleAccessibility conformance, Thai language, mobile support and automated testing in place before wider rollout.
ContinuityDocumentation and handover material sufficient for another engineer to take over — the organisation should not be dependent on one person.

One item to settle in writing

The Growth Hub was built on personal time, personal equipment and personal subscriptions, outside my job description. I would like the ownership position recorded clearly and agreed by both sides before the platform holds real student data — not because I expect it to be contentious, but because it is far easier to settle now than later. I am glad to take whatever form the organisation prefers, including full assignment as part of a contracted role.

What I am asking for now

  • Approval to run the pilot described in the accompanying summary
  • Agreement in principle to this role, with terms to be worked through
  • A conversation about time allocation and compensation before the pilot begins, rather than after

If the organisation's answer is that this is not a direction it wants to pursue, that is a legitimate outcome and I would rather know now. The platform works either way; the question is whether it is useful here.
