Addendum to the Implementation Plan

Course catalogue and public guidebook

To: David Doran, Founder & CEO  ·  From: Mani Golzary, Platform Lead  ·  Date: 25 September 2026  ·  Accompanies: Implementation Plan, version 2

Why this addendum

After reviewing the walkthrough of our previous WeLearn course system, the plan now also includes our course catalogue: around 1,030 courses from partner schools such as Florida Virtual School and Brigham Young University, a full course editor for staff, and a public course guidebook that families can search. The updated plan (version 2) is attached.
