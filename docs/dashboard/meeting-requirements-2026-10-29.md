## Requirements applied in this dashboard pass

- Make My Dashboard the student's hub and default destination. Preserve intentional deep links and other roles' existing landing behaviour.
- Prioritise the student's current learning. Put academic courses on the left and current STEM/PBL on the right on desktop; stack on mobile.
- Show only the learner's enrolled/assigned PBL programs in the current-project area. Keep broad discovery secondary.
- Combine Today, Next Step and Goals in one learning-plan section, with Today, Ahead and Completed views. Keep completed goals instead of automatically deleting them.
- Keep course-session completion distinct from the separate term-project checklist. Do not present opening a STEAM activity as academic mastery.
- Provide visible working links to courses, projects, evidence/portfolio, goal management, discovery and general future-skills learning.
- Keep notes, reflection, music/focus tools, badges and other optional tools accessible with less prominence.
- Use existing account-scoped saves and progress readers. Example academic lessons are clearly labelled, optional and never persisted into student records.

## Discussed but requiring later product and data work

- Goals → Quests → Tasks terminology across the wider application; the meeting initially used inconsistent meanings before proposing this hierarchy.
- Accurate Today / This Week / End of Term filtering needs authoritative dates, tasks and scheduling data. Ahead currently means all remaining personal goals; it does not invent deadlines.
- Current-course competencies and future skills should reflect the individual PBL and learner. Full level rubrics, coach assessment, evidence requirements and personalised progression need their own contract.
- An achievement history across completed courses, PBL, feedback and transcripts needs a dedicated history source. This pass retains completed goals and the existing portfolio links.
- Mastery Transcript/Mastery.org connection and AI recommendations are planned, not verified integrations.
- A grid/slide preference was discussed. This pass follows the user's explicit fixed desktop two-column layout and responsive mobile layout; the existing optional tools and appearance preferences remain available.
- Dedicated curriculum-authoring and commercial school/subscription tools are later work.
