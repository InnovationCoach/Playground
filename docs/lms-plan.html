1. Summary

WeLearn Growth Hub will be a single learning platform for all of our cohorts. Students open their Edmentum courses, the WeLearn PBL/STEAM simulations and other materials in one place, and staff, teachers and parents each see what they need.

CohortLearnersWhat the platform provides for them
W2ETwice-exceptional (2e) learners, secondaryA learning profile per student with accommodations (extended time, reduced visual load, text-to-speech, tasks broken into steps), stretch challenges that build on strengths, and support notes visible only to that student's teachers.
WPRInternational students, primaryA simpler, age-appropriate interface; accounts activated only after verified parental consent; English/Chinese/Thai throughout; AI tutor limited or off by default for younger children.
All cohortsEvery learnerOne sign-in, My Courses, gradebook and transcripts, parent portal, mobile-friendly and accessible design.

Dual programme: Thai Diploma. Every cohort can also take the Thai Diploma alongside their main programme. It is taught by a Thai Diploma teacher: students open WeLearn Growth Hub and access the learning materials, lessons and assignments the teacher publishes, in Thai. Each student's record shows their main programme and the Thai Diploma side by side, with a separate Thai Diploma transcript.

Recommendation. Build these LMS functions into WeLearn itself and roll them out in stages, starting with a one-class pilot. The build takes an estimated 5–6 months, needs no outside development company, and needs no LMS licence. In exchange, the school takes on ownership: a named staff member, a hosting bill, and a security review before launch.

What we (the build team) deliver

  • Admin console: staff, student and parent accounts, schools, classes
  • Student "My Courses" home: Edmentum, simulations and materials together
  • One-click Edmentum launch with grades returned (LTI 1.3)
  • Teacher gradebook, terms and transcripts
  • Public course guidebook: our full catalogue of partner-school courses, searchable by families
  • Parent portal, English/Chinese/Thai, mobile-friendly

What the school must provide

  • Approval of this plan and funding for its costs
  • Confirmation of the Platform Lead role, with protected time
  • Firebase Blaze billing plan
  • A sending mailbox in our existing Microsoft 365
  • Edmentum registration details for WeLearn (LTI 1.3)
  • A security & privacy review before launch

3. Review of the supplied wireframes

The 20 "EduBiz Gateway System" wireframes were reviewed. They are a good specification for the admin side, but they describe an education-agency back office (enquiries, course bookings, accreditations, student balances), not an LMS. They contain no student course view, no Edmentum launch, no assignments and no gradebook, which are the parts students and teachers use most.

DecisionWireframe screens
KeepStaff / student / parent account lists, create, edit, suspend, account history, change email, settings, password reset, language settings (extended to English, Chinese and Thai).
RedesignParent linking (typing an email lets anyone attach to any child → replace with one-time invite codes). Student photos (optional, off by default, location data stripped). Public "Client Viewable" reviews (moderated, with consent, or removed).
Keep, with the catalogueEducation Providers, Accreditations and course reviews: part of the course catalogue and public guidebook (Phase C).
Later (Phase G)Enquiries, Course Bookings and student balances: part of buying courses and study paths after go-live.
AddMy Courses, Edmentum launch, assignments, gradebook, terms/transcripts, teacher class view, parent portal.

Data inconsistencies in the mock-ups have been fixed in our designs. Examples: the same User ID assigned to three different people, a parent shown with 0 students who has 2, and "page 1 of 3" for 129 records. One mock-up student is 12: with the WPR primary cohort, under-13 learners are in scope, and the plan provides the parental consent this requires (Phase E).

4. How it fits together

LTI 1.3 is the published education standard that lets an LMS open an outside course tool with one click (no second login) and receive grades back. Edmentum states it supports "any LMS that supports LTI 1.3". WeLearn will implement the LMS side of that standard.

6. Implementation phases

Durations are estimates for the build team and assume the school's inputs (section 7) arrive on time. Edmentum registration runs in parallel and should be started early.

Phase 0 · Front-end groundworkWeeks 1–4 · Can start now
  • Admin console layout with WeLearn branding, role-aware navigation and a reusable data table
  • Staff, student and parent account screens, built against test data until the backend is live
  • Settings (change password, language) and password reset. These work for real immediately.
  • First version of the student "My Courses" page and the parent portal screens
Phase A · Backend, roles, cohorts and schools3–4 weeks once Blaze is on
  • Deploy the backend server; the AI tutor becomes live in production
  • Roles: admin, supervisor, course coordinator (teacher), student, parent
  • Organisation → cohorts (W2E, WPR) → schools → classes structure, with a safe migration of existing accounts
  • Dual enrolment: any student in any cohort can also be enrolled in the Thai Diploma
  • Admin actions (create, edit, suspend, change email) with a tamper-proof account history
Needs: Blaze plan · Microsoft 365 sending mailbox · Platform Lead approval before production migration
Phase B · Edmentum connection (LTI 1.3)3–4 weeks
  • Built and tested first against the free official LTI reference tool, so work is not blocked on Edmentum
  • One-click launch from My Courses; teachers place Edmentum content into WeLearn courses
  • Edmentum scores flow back into WeLearn automatically
Needs: Edmentum registration details · confirmation that licences cover our students
Phase C · Course catalogue, My Courses and cohort experiencesabout 5 weeks
  • Courses, subjects and year levels as data, not hard-coded pages
  • Each course has a provider (Edmentum, WeLearn PBL, or teacher-led) and curriculum standards (e.g. NGSS, and the Thai national curriculum for the Thai Diploma)
  • Standards-coverage report for leadership
  • Full course editor: education provider, year levels, subjects, length, delivery type, credits, prerequisites, fees, accreditations, teachers, "featured", and a review status, with every text field translatable
  • Import of our existing catalogue of about 1,030 partner-school courses into a review queue: AI drafts the clean-up and Thai/Chinese translations, and a staff member approves each course
  • Public course guidebook: search and filters (provider, subject, grade, accreditation, length) and a page for every course
  • Primary-friendly student interface for WPR: larger targets, simpler navigation, read-aloud
  • W2E learning profiles and accommodations applied automatically across courses and simulations
  • Teacher-led courses: teachers upload learning materials (documents, videos, links), organise them into units and lessons, and set assignments. The Thai Diploma is the first, with a full Thai-language interface
Phase D · Gradebook, terms and transcriptsabout 4 weeks
  • Terms (the wireframes' "transcript periods"), teacher gradebook combining Edmentum scores and PBL rubric scores
  • Transcript export per student: main programme and a separate Thai Diploma transcript in the format it requires
Phase E · Parent portal and parental consentabout 3 weeks
  • Verified parental consent before any primary (WPR) account is activated, with consent records kept
  • Parents link to a child with a one-time invite code from the school; read-only progress and grades
  • Optional weekly email summary in English or Chinese
Phase F · Launch readiness2–3 weeks
  • Independent security test and fixes; privacy review; staff training material
  • Pilot with one W2E class first; WPR primary classes join once parental consent is live; then all cohorts after sign-off
Phase G · Buy Course and study paths (after go-live)3–4 weeks, after the pilot
  • "Buy Course" on the guidebook: online payment or an enquiry to our team, as the school decides
  • "Add to Path": each student's personal study plan combining courses, e.g. partner-school courses alongside the Thai Diploma
  • Enquiries and course bookings managed in the admin console
